WAITHAKA NDEGWA WEBSITE — CRON JOBS
===================================

cPanel username: afrie015

REQUIRED CRON JOBS: NONE

This build does not require a recurring cron job.
- Scheduled posts become visible when their publish time is reached because visibility is evaluated on request.
- Sitemap and RSS are generated dynamically.
- M-Pesa, Pesapal and PayPal use callbacks/IPN/return flows.

Do not add a made-up maintenance cron unless a dedicated CLI maintenance script is added in a future build.
