# Enterprise areas implementation — 25 October 2026

Implements the persistent enterprise-profile portion of the earlier enterprise/opportunity design. The private Enterprise portfolio supports identity, sector, stage, operating area, establishment date, logo upload, problem, approach, customers, Waithaka's involvement, dated numeric targets, public successes, public challenges/responses, next steps, linked articles and separate private evidence/permission notes.

Profiles use encrypted settings records, owner-only mutations, advisory locks and optimistic versions. Saving a working copy cannot replace the public snapshot. Publication requires an explicit claims review, permission confirmation and a private permission reference. Public output is a whitelist, not the full private record. Unpublishing removes public visibility; mutation actor/version metadata and prior approved snapshots remain private.

Public directory: /enterprises, with name/purpose, sector, country and stage filters. Individual profiles have stable URLs, in-page section links and related published articles only. Draft/future scheduled articles remain hidden. Published profiles and the directory enter the sitemap. No fictional ventures, employees, success results or baseline zeroes are seeded. The Goal2030 target of 30 Goblis Group startups remains distinct from 2,030 Ubunifu incubated enterprises.

Targets stay labelled targets. Actual aggregate reporting still requires approved source-system evidence. These enterprise areas do not bypass the existing monthly reporting contract, import private producer data, or publish unreviewed metrics. First real profiles require owner content and permission.

The separate founder-approved employment-opportunity submission/review/publication workflow remains in the earlier design and is not claimed implemented by this profile release.
