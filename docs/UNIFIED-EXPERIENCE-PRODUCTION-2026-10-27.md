## Contact, suitability and repeat posting

The `/promote` contact step now includes country (Kenya default), phone calling code (+254 default), phone, County, Sub-County and Town/Area. International residence uses its regional equivalents, independently of telephone code. Contact data is private and excluded from article/AI facts. The completed-step review shows the active entered values without interpreting user-supplied HTML.

New advertising proposals require explicit suitability assessment; payment is not approval. The owner can approve the reviewed scope, request evidence/changes or decline. Content/creative changes invalidate the review.

The owner opens a sponsored feature in Publishing desk and selects **Open suitability & repeat controls**. A complete exact campaign review pins all captions, assets, accounts and dates. The first contractual round is launched explicitly. Only after both first-round placements have recorded valid publication links can subsequent approved rounds be scheduled automatically.

This release does not silently enable every historical campaign. Payment, client and owner approval, contract, current source, offer validity, destination and global delivery controls remain required. Connected supported routes can deliver automatically; assisted accounts remain assisted. An uncertain result, cancellation, refund, changed material or obsolete date stops future automatic work for review. No renewal or additional charge is created.
