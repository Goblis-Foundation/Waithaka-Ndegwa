# Enterprise areas and founder-approved employment opportunities
Research and implementation design · 25 October 2026

## Scope and identity
Create a persistent public area for each of Goblis Group’s 30 target ventures. Preserve the separate Ubunifu Institute target of 2,030 incubated social enterprises. Do not claim that target ventures already exist, or seed fictional operational results. A venture may link to a Startup Journey without replacing the current featured-journey model.

## Enterprise area
Each approved profile has a stable URL, identity/logo, sector, country/operating area, enterprise stage, establishment date, problem, solution, beneficiaries/customers, responsible organisation, founder consent, and a clear explanation of Waithaka’s involvement.
Tabs: Overview; Targets & progress; Successes & learning; Challenges & responses; Articles & milestones; Opportunities.
Search/filter by sector, country, stage and theme. Mobile cards expose identity, current stage, latest dated update and next target; full details remain readable without charts.

## Evidence and targets
Each metric records definition, unit, baseline value/date, target value/date, actual value, measurement period, source system, evidence, responsible reviewer and publication decision. Separate measurement dates from approval/publication dates and connection checks. The existing monthly reporting contract remains authoritative.
Show permanent employees and FTE separately; distinguish direct employment from employment in incubated enterprises; explain indirect beneficiaries, estimation method, assumptions and overlap. Unknown values say not reported. Targets are labelled targets; no invented progress bars.
Publish approved aggregates only from Goblis Group, Ubunifu Institute, AfriTrace Link or Goblis Foundation. A public profile must never reveal private producer records or confidential challenges.
A success entry describes what changed, for whom, how much, when, evidence, the enterprise’s contribution and limitations. A challenge entry records issue, effect, response, owner, next review and lessons, with separate public/private versions.

## Articles
A many-to-many enterprise/article link associates existing published My Thoughts articles. Drafts and private research never appear publicly. Articles can have an update type, topic, measurement period and related milestone. Show chronological progress with success and challenge filters. Unpublishing an article removes it from the public enterprise area.

## Employment opportunity submission
Founder submits organisation identity and verified business contact, relationship to enterprise, role title, purpose, responsibilities, essential/desirable criteria, number of positions, employment type, permanent/temporary status, workplace mode, country/city or eligible remote locations, compensation range/currency/period or an explicit reason if not disclosed, application destination, closing date/timezone, accessibility arrangements and application process.
Separate genuine job opportunities from volunteering, internships and contractor assignments; label paid/unpaid accurately. No application fee. Do not collect CVs, IDs or applicant-sensitive information through this listing flow.
Offer a private save-and-resume link and accessible mobile form. Proof of authority stays private.

## Review and approval
States: submitted → identity/details review → clarification requested → revised submission → editorial review → founder approval pending → founder approved → scheduled/published → closed/expired. Rejected/withdrawn remain auditable and private.
Questions are explicit, numbered and tied to a version; founder responds via expiring one-use email link and can attach requested evidence privately. Review history records actor/date/reason. Staff may edit for clarity, but every substantive change invalidates prior approval.
Send the exact frozen listing to the founder for approval by email; offer a prepared WhatsApp approval link using the existing verified delivery route. No approval by email-open, link preview, or GET. Explicit signed-token POST approves the exact digest/version. Staff publication remains separate and is blocked until founder approval.
Reuse the existing maximum-three-review convention: one review round is one complete returned founder-review package; clarifications before that package do not silently consume a round. Explain the remaining rounds. On third non-approval, close without publication; retain the private audit record. Provide withdrawal and reporting controls.
Employment eligibility and candidate selection remain with the employer, outside this website.

## Publication and maintenance
Enterprise-specific opportunity pages show organisation, role, location/mode, compensation disclosure, date posted, closing date, application instructions and source/review date. Automatically close expired listings; remove application CTA and JobPosting markup immediately. Renewals require fresh founder approval if content or dates change. No guarantee of employment or inferred employer endorsement.
Only live, complete individual job pages receive JobPosting JSON-LD. Exclude category pages, drafts, unpaid-volunteer listings and expired jobs. Do not imply that website publication automatically posts a job to LinkedIn or Meta; social derivatives enter the existing approval/scheduling system.

## Dashboard
Enterprise portfolio overview; profile/evidence editor; linked-article picker; milestone calendar; opportunity review inbox with needs-information, awaiting-founder, expiring-soon and closed filters; exact-version comparison; notification audit; API aggregate freshness. Separate public preview from internal notes.

## Acceptance criteria
- Thirty target ventures are not confused with 2,030 Ubunifu enterprises.
- Every public value has a measured period and approved source; absent data remains absent.
- Related article visibility follows original publication status.
- No opportunity publishes without the founder approving the exact final version.
- Revisions invalidate approval; repeated/expired/foreign tokens cannot approve.
- Deadline closure removes applications and job schema.
- No candidate applications, private evidence or personal details become public.
