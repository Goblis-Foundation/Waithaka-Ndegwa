# Opportunity editorial standard

Each opportunity is a separate, practical guide, not a copied promotional announcement or a combined roundup. Preserve its stable URL, original publication date, category and correct funding classification.

## Required reader information

Explain the purpose and provider, actual benefits, eligible countries and applicant stage, official eligibility requirements, deadline with honest timezone precision, application destination, costs and obligations, and material limitations. Distinguish pooled prizes from individual awards, debt from grants, services from unrestricted cash, stipends from project funding and introductions from guaranteed investment.

Add tailored decision support: who should consider the opportunity, a preparation checklist, how to present evidence, and the practical commitment involved. Label editorial suggestions clearly; do not invent official document requirements, scoring criteria, fees, deadlines or benefits to make a listing longer. Aim for substantive guidance, usually 500-800 words when evidence and useful advice justify it, not filler to satisfy a word quota.

Use current organiser sources and preserve unresolved discrepancies prominently. Never turn a research limitation into a claim that the opportunity is certainly open. Keep sensitive-document submissions on the organiser's verified route. Remove obsolete internal research footers; source attribution and source-check dates belong in the existing source section.

## Reader support

Show an optional 'Support this work' message after the provider's application section on independent public opportunity pages and below the opportunities directory. Link to the existing /contribute journey. The contribution supports Waithaka Ndegwa's research and publishing; it is not an application fee, is not paid to the opportunity provider and has no effect on access, eligibility or selection. Never gate applications, preselect a payment, collect payment on these pages, or add the request to paid sponsored features or private previews.

## Release safeguards

Use native Writer drafts and exact-version Publishing Desk approval/publication. Preserve subsequent editorial edits, closed and withdrawn listings, original publication dates, slugs and existing payment/provider settings. Store an idempotent release marker; do not duplicate posts, auto-send newsletters or create social jobs. Verify all public pages, provider links, support destination, mobile layout, sitemap and the unchanged article-free homepage.
