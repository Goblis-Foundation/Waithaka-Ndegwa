# Booking contacts
New meeting choices: Google Meet (default), Phone / WhatsApp (one option), Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Other. Signal is removed from new bookings. Historical records retain their original method and contact; none are rewritten or cancelled.

Phone number is visible for every method, required for Phone / WhatsApp. Calling code starts +254, Country starts Kenya, City is blank/editable; all international values can be changed. Kenya local leading0 is normalised for +254; a full international +number overrides the default. Country supports suggestions and free text for territories. Contacts/location stay in private booking review and are excluded from AI context. No geolocation is collected and selecting WhatsApp never sends a message by itself.
