## Suitability before money or publication

New sponsored records begin with a pending suitability review. The owner records the category, evidence/rights note and explicit approval, requested changes or decline. Payment cannot satisfy this gate. Source or creative changes invalidate exact-version suitability. Existing previously reviewed records retain their historical safeguards until edited; they are not silently reclassified or charged.

The public policy explains relevance, truthful claims, safety, rights and disclosure. Excluded offers are refused at intake. Regulated services require appropriate evidence and may be declined; the software does not decide legal eligibility or substitute an automatic keyword filter for editorial judgement.

Enabling it freezes every revision and account binding, then an explicit action launches the first contractual round. Under the existing packages one round is one post on each of two agreed accounts.

Only after both first-round jobs have actual valid publication links can the bounded worker schedule subsequent approved rounds. All payment, public-source, client factual approval, owner approval, suitability, contract, offer expiry and account checks are re-evaluated. No new charge, renewal, provider permission or substitute account is created. Unsupported/manual destinations remain assisted. The existing posting worker's overall pause and connection controls remain authoritative.

Changed content, cancellation, refunds, disputes, obsolete dates or uncertain outcomes stop future repeat work for review. There is no catch-up blast, replay of uncertain posts or duplication of a partially delivered round. Confirmed remote evidence is retained. Enabling the feature does not activate it for every historical campaign.
